About me

I'm Vijay Kothare, a UX designer in Mumbai with more than thirteen years of turning complex problems into simple, human products.

Hi, I'm Vijay 👋
My story

I hold a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Applied Arts, which gave me strong design fundamentals and a habit of asking 'why' before 'how'.

Since then I've designed across a wide range of domains: healthcare, education, food, insurance, travel, the creator economy and B2B manufacturing. I keep my skills in step with current standards and design trends, but the core stays the same: start with people, then design for them.

Every project is a chance to push further. I aim for solutions that go beyond what the brief asked for, so the result is good to look at and easy to use.

When I'm not designing, I'm usually on my motorcycle exploring new roads and photographing what I find. I paint on my iPad as I travel, share photographs on X, document journeys on my YouTube channel, and sell my art at The Soul Strokes.

What I believe

Three things I won't skip

Human first

Listen before designing

Interviews, empathy maps and real conversations come before a single pixel.

Pixel picky

Details are the design

Contrast, spacing and the small states (empty, error, success) are where trust is won.

No templates

Every brief is new

I build from the problem up, so each product looks and works like itself.

FAQ

Questions I often get

What kind of projects do you take on?

UX and UI for mobile apps and websites, brand identities and logos, packaging, social media campaigns and motion. Most projects combine two or more of these.

How do you start a project?

By listening. I begin with interviews or a workshop to understand the people involved, their goals and frustrations, then map the journey before designing a single screen.

Can I see your research, not just final screens?

Yes. The FoodFeed and Edumentr case studies show personas, flows, wireframes, testing and the changes made from feedback.

Do you prototype?

Always. Clickable prototypes let clients and users try the product before it's built. Two of them run live on this site.

Where are you based?

Mumbai, India. I work with clients across India and abroad, including Sydney-based Tumble Tale.
