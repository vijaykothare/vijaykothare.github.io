Branding · 2014

What's Hot

A fast food brand for young India, from logo to ordering app.

Client
Concept brand
My role
Brand identity, web & app design
Platform
Brand, print, web, mobile
Year
2014
Overview

What's Hot is a fast food brand concept for India, built around what young people want now: quick, healthy and flexible food, veg and non-veg, affordable and tasty, with flavours from around the world.

Deliverables
Logo & palettePackaging & printWebsiteMobile app
The briefIdentityBrand in useWebsiteMobile app

01The brief

They are looking for what's new. They are looking for What's Hot.

Young Indians care more about what they eat and where it comes from. They want global flavours that still suit their palates, available whenever they want.

02Identity

A hand-lettered wordmark built on a construction grid, with colour inversions for every surface and a palette of greens, warm yellows, chilli reds and earthy browns.

03Brand in use

Stationery, takeaway boxes and bags in kraft paper, and 'Tear, Scan, Find out' posters that link print to the app.

04Website

  • A first-time vs returning visitor split on the home page: pick up or delivery, city and location
  • Cuisine selector, menu grids with ratings, and an 'Add extras' customiser
  • A visible cart with totals and a 'Redeem your coupon' moment

05Mobile app

The same playful, illustrated world in a mobile ordering app: cuisines, item detail and cart.
