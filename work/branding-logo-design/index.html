InsureJoy

An insurance brand that feels like joy, not paperwork.

Client
InsureJoy and others
My role
Brand identity & logo design
Platform
Print, digital, merchandise
Year
2022
Overview

InsureJoy is an insurance platform. The identity centres on a leaping figure that also reads as the 'i' of the name, turning a category usually associated with fine print into something optimistic.

Visit insurejoy.com ↗
Deliverables
Logo & wordmarkStationeryMerchandiseLogo portfolio
The ideaBrand in useMore logo work

01The idea

A person mid-jump, arms open: the relief of being covered.

The mark doubles as the first letter of the wordmark, so symbol and name always work together. A confident royal blue with a lighter sky tone keeps it trustworthy while the figure keeps it human.

02Brand in use

The identity was rolled out across stationery, ID cards, merchandise and the website, and the mark stays legible from a lanyard to a T-shirt.

03More logo work

Identities for La Tienda, Capitalgram, Able Studios, Dhruv Engineering, Insuregram, HealthyBraja, Vaishnavi Foods and Novato.
