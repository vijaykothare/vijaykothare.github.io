Health Plan Member App

Health records, claims and plan contacts in a member's pocket.

Client
Health plan provider
My role
UX & UI design
Platform
Mobile app
Year
2015
Overview

A mobile app for members of an integrated care health plan. It brings health news, useful links, plan contacts, personal health records and authorisation and claims search into one secure app.

Deliverables
Information architectureMobile UISearch flows
The problemKey design decisionsVisual system

01The problem

Members had to call or navigate long websites to find a provider, check an authorisation or see their own care plan. Much of the audience is not tech-savvy, so every task needed to be obvious.

02Key design decisions

01Welcome before loginHealth news, useful links and one-tap Contact Us and Email Us are available without signing in.
02Records as an icon gridCare visits, conditions, medicines, care plan, service plan and contingency plan are each one tap away.
03Tasks in one menuSearch authorisations, search claims, health tools, help and FAQ live together.
04Forms that guideClear labels, date pickers and a single primary button on every search.

03Visual system

A calm, clinical teal with generous white space and large touch targets keeps information easy to read, while family photography keeps it human.
