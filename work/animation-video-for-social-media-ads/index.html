Motion for Social

Short, punchy animated spots built for the first three seconds of a scroll.

Client
The Great Indian Pizza and others
My role
Motion design & animation
Platform
Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
Year
2023
Overview

Animated social video ads, including work for The Great Indian Pizza ('Crust Us..!!!'). Each spot is designed to work with the sound off and to land the brand and offer within the first seconds.

Deliverables
Animated adsVertical & square cuts
ApproachWatch

01Approach

  • Hook in the first frame: bold brand colour and type, with motion that starts immediately
  • Kinetic typography that carries the message without audio
  • Vertical and square cuts for stories, reels and feed
  • Brand assets turned into motion: the pizza mark, the yellow and orange palette, playful copy
