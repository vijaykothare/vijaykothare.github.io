Taruveda Xtracts

A B2B website that answers a technical buyer's questions before they ask them.

Client
Taruveda Xtracts, Satara
My role
UX strategy, UI design, B2B website
Platform
Responsive website
Year
2026
Overview

Taruveda Xtracts produces essential oils, oleoresins and floral extracts using supercritical CO₂ at its plant in Satara, Maharashtra. Its buyers are flavour and fragrance houses, FMCG and nutraceutical manufacturers who judge a supplier on numbers, documents and consistency. I designed a site that reads like a technical data sheet but feels premium enough for fragrance brands.

01Context

Supercritical CO₂ extraction leaves no solvent residue, runs at low temperature and can target specific compounds. For buyers that matters, but only if they can verify it. Procurement and R&D teams shortlist suppliers quickly and drop any site that hides its specifications behind a contact form.

02The problem

A buyer should be able to check capability, compare products and request a sample in one visit.

  • The technology is unfamiliar to many buyers and needs a plain explanation
  • Specifications are dense and hard to scan
  • Different buyers (F&F, FMCG, nutraceutical, export) care about different proof
  • The brand had to feel premium, not like a commodity trader

03Who it's for

R&D and formulationNeed actives, standardisation and residue data.
ProcurementNeed capacity, consistency, COA and lead times.
Export & trading housesNeed reliable volume and documentation.

04Structure & flow

  • Hero: the promise and four proof points (up to 2 MT/month, 300–600 bar, <0.1% residual CO₂, COA every batch)
  • Why CO₂: one paragraph on the science, then six benefit cards
  • Portfolio: eighteen botanicals in three families
  • Buyers, quality, sourcing, then a sample and COA request

05Key design decisions

01Numbers in the first screenCapacity, pressure, residue and COA sit directly under the headline, because that is what a buyer checks first.
02Plain-language science'Solvent extraction gives you the compound. CO₂ gives you the plant.' turns a technical process into a clear benefit.
03A repeatable product cardEvery botanical shows its Latin name, form (oleoresin, essential oil, absolute) and standardised actives in the same order, so products can be compared quickly.
04One clear action'Request a sample & COA' is the only primary action, repeated at the points where a buyer is most convinced.

06Mobile

Specification-heavy content reflows into single cards so buyers on the move, at trade shows or on the factory floor, can still check a product and send a request.

07Visual system

Earthy neutrals, botanical photography and a refined serif tie the brand to the plant world. Gold is reserved for actions. Dark sections carry the quality and sourcing story, where trust matters most.

08Outcome

A credible, specification-first site where a technical buyer can verify capability, compare eighteen botanicals and request a sample with a Certificate of Analysis in one visit.
