Social · 2024

Social Media Post Design

Travel creatives that sell a trip in the time it takes to scroll past it.

Client
Samrut Holidays
My role
Art direction & social design
Platform
Instagram & Facebook
Year
2024
Overview

A series of social ads for Samrut Holidays, a travel company selling tours across India and abroad: Rajasthan heritage circuits, the Andaman Islands, Kashmir, Ramayana Yatra pilgrimages, luxury stays and cruises.

Deliverables
Feed postsCarouselsStoriesReusable templates
The problemA repeatable systemSelected creativesOutcome

01The problem

Travel feeds are crowded with beautiful photos. Each post had to show the place, the itinerary, the price hook and the contact details without becoming cluttered, and still look like one brand across dozens of posts.

02A repeatable system

  • Headline, hero photo, destination arches or photo cards, itinerary line, and a fixed contact footer
  • Itineraries written as scannable night counts (2N Jaipur – 1N Bikaner – 2N Jaisalmer …)
  • A '0% EMI, No Down Payment' badge as a consistent offer device
  • Palettes taken from each destination: sunset for Rajasthan, ocean blue for cruises, cream and gold for luxury

03Selected creatives

04Outcome

A flexible social system the team can reuse for new destinations, keeping the brand recognisable while each trip gets its own mood.
