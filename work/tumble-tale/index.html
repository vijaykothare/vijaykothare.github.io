Tumble Tale

Personalised storybooks where a child becomes the hero of their own adventure.

Client
Tumble Tale, Sydney
My role
UX & UI design, interactive prototype
Platform
Desktop web
Year
2024
Overview

Tumble Tale creates personalised digital and audio stories in which the child is the main character. Parents and grandparents choose a theme, characters and tone, and receive a one-of-a-kind story to read at bedtime. I designed the storefront and the story creation flow, and built the clickable prototype you can try below.

01The problem

A personalised product is hard to picture before you buy it.

Parents need to trust that the story will really be about their child, not a template with a name pasted in. The process also has to feel easy enough to finish in a few minutes, often on a tired evening.

02Who it's for

ParentsLooking for a bedtime ritual that feels special.
Gift giversGrandparents, aunts and uncles searching for something thoughtful.
Digital familiesReading on a tablet or listening to audio, not only in print.

03Key design decisions

01A promise, then one button'Where Your Imagination Becomes the Story' with a single bright 'Get Started' keeps the first step obvious.
02Three steps, illustrated'How it works' (You choose, We create, You enjoy) answers 'what happens after I pay?' before it's asked.
03Social proof from real roles'Story Time Smiles' testimonials come from a mom, an uncle and a grandma, mirroring the three buyer types.
04Simple choiceTwo products, One Tumble Tale and a 3-Story Bundle, with price and Buy Now side by side.
05Reward for subscribing10% off the first purchase gives a reason to leave an email.

04Visual system

Soft indigo, sunshine yellow and leafy green, wavy section edges and hand-drawn doodles give the site a storybook feel. Rounded display type and large buttons keep it friendly for children while staying clear for the adults who buy.

05Outcome

A warm, conversion-focused storefront with a complete clickable prototype covering the landing page, story creation and checkout. Try it below: it runs live on this page.
